title: A new home for Noctrinth
summary: The site has a front page, news of its own and the changelog on a page of its own.
date: 2026-10-15T12:00:00+09:00
---

Until now this site was one long page of release notes. It is now three things.

## The front page

What Noctrinth is, the download for your computer and what it adds over the Modrinth App, in English and Russian. The download button picks the installer for your system from the latest release.

## News

Articles like this one: what is coming, what changed and why. They are in Russian and English, and the launcher shows them in its News sidebar next to Modrinth's, so you do not need to visit the site to read them.

## The changelog

Every Noctrinth and Modrinth App release note, as before, now with a link to each version and a filter for one or the other.

If something on the site is wrong or missing, [open an issue](https://github.com/Everelsu/Noctrinth/issues).
